### Summary

- **The order the question presupposes.** On the texts read here, the order physics calls causal (earlier matter producing later matter) is not among the Paṭṭhāna's 24 conditions. The commentaries file it under a catch-all they define only by co-variation.
- **What physics would have to add.** To host the rest, physics needs a causal order with:
  - sorts (mental and material);
  - links defined relative to a sort;
  - internal structure at each element for relations among things arisen together;
  - object edges that are not edges of the order at all.
- **Measurement.** Only one place gives a measurement: emergence from cessation, and future objects with it.

### 2. The texts' own configuration (read first)

- **T1 — what each condition relates.**
  - The proximity group always conditions citta and cetasika (`abh03m7.mul.xml:97`, `:133`, `:241`, `:245`).
  - The negative method says matter of every kind arises *naanantarapaccayā*, "not by proximity" (`:1201`, "like not-object"; the list at `:1161` covers mind-born, kamma-born, external, temperature-born and the mindless beings' matter).
  - The 24 give matter-to-matter edges only as:
    - co-nascent within one cluster (great elements to each other and to derived matter; material life faculty to kamma-born matter);
    - edible food to "this body" (`:137–257`; Saṅgaha §28–34, `abh07t.nrf.xml:2909–2941`). Food is the one edge that reaches *other* clusters (`abh08t.nrf.xml:7465`).
- **T1′ — production of matter by earlier matter is outside the 24.**
  - The ṭīkās say this: a decisive support "not come in the Paṭṭhāna" (*paṭṭhāne anāgato*) holds "by the Suttanta method, *sati bhāvā asati ca abhāvā*" (when it is, that is; when it is not, that is not). It is called *upanissayakoṭi* (`e0104n.att.xml:4401`, which adds a reported *kira* saying: "decisive support is the great reference of conditions"; `abh02t.tik.xml:1957`).
  - The Vibhāvinī uses exactly this for matter: external temperature and nutriment "having obtained decisive support … originate matter" (`abh07t.nrf.xml:6201`).
- **T2 — two kinds of loop.** The Mūlaṭīkā says "helpers of their helper" by pre- and post-nascence (the heart-base and the mental aggregates) are *not* mutuality (*na ca taṃ icchitaṃ*). Mutuality needs relata that arose together (`abh03t.tik.xml:4317`; `e0104n.att.xml:3581`). The Vibhāvinī places post-nascence's support on the body's role as cause of its series *continuing* (`:6717`), which runs forward in time.
- **T3 — two voices on the gap (A112).**
  - Buddhaghosa rejects the teachers' temporal reading of contiguity (`abh03a.att.xml:7697`; `e0102n.mul.xml:3701`). The Visuddhimagga-ṭīkā names those teachers as Revata, and glosses his "we say this too" as "there IS a temporal interval, seven days or so" (`e0104n.att.xml:3569`).
  - The ṭīkās' own analysis: "time is designated only on the occurrence of dhammas", so "there is no time-interval". Matter "makes no interval, being another series", and absence makes none (`abh03t.tik.xml:4309`; `e0104n.att.xml:3573`).
  - The Vibhāvinī: matter, "of a different kind, cannot intervene" (`:6653`; it reports the temporal reading as *keci* and rejects it).
- **T4 — conditions do nothing.** "Capacity to produce", "since dhammas have no activity" (*abyāpārattā*), is the mode under which, when X has ceased or is present, Y-type dhammas come to be (`abh03t.tik.xml:4309`). This is a regularity, not a transmission.
- **T5 — objects need not exist.** "There is no dhamma that does not become object" (`abh07t.nrf.xml:6649`). Future aggregates condition knowledge of the future as object (`abh03m8.mul.xml:14089`). The Kathāvatthu, against the Andhakas: no knowledge of the immediately next future; the commentary allows knowledge of a future "with interval" (`abh03a.att.xml:4013`).
- **T6 — what ends cessation** (Visuddhimagga XXIII):
  - the fixed time;
  - impending death ("he emerges in between", `:8469`; "it is said", *vuttaṃ*, that this must be adverted);
  - a monk standing "within earshot" (*savanūpacāre*) saying "the Sangha awaits you" — "by the mere saying there is emergence; so weighty is the Sangha's command" (`:8457`);
  - with prior adverting, he emerges "at that time" the Sangha wishes to act, *before* anyone comes to call (`:8453`).

### 3. Derivations

**D1 — Physical causal order is not among the 24.**
- *Statement.* If A0, A78, A102, A6 and T1, then no edge among the 24 runs forward in time between external material events. Physics' causal order lands in the texts' *upanissayakoṭi*, which has no order axioms.
- *Maths.* Let E be the edges whose relata are both external matter. Then ∀(x,y,c) ∈ E: rise(x) = rise(y) ∧ locus(x) = locus(y). So ≺_BLMS ∩ E_time = ∅, and matter's production maps to U, where x U y ⟺ (x ⇒ y) ∧ (¬x ⇒ ¬y).
- *What physics adds.* A locally finite partial order (Bombelli, Lee, Meyer and Sorkin 1987) can be the structure of U without conflicting with any line read.
- **EXTENDS.** Measurement: none — philosophy.

**D2 — The pre/post "two-cycle" unrolls into a chain.**
- *Statement.* If A87, A88 and the Vibhāvinī's reading, the heart-base/aggregates pair is a feedback chain in time, not a cycle.
- *Maths.* Take events = (dhamma, moment of exercise) and φ(x,t) = 2t + [x is mental]. Then (base,t) → (citta,t) → (body,t′) with t′ > t, so φ rises strictly along every edge and the subgraph is acyclic.
- **AGREES** with any causal order (loops unrolled in time).
- *The other option is open.* Read both as presence-mode at one t, and you get a synchronic 2-cycle among co-present relata. The Mūlaṭīkā declines to call that mutuality. So the chain is not forced.
- Measurement: none — a modelling choice.

**D3 — Mutuality needs structure inside an element.**
- *Statement.* If A83 and A84, every synchronic edge found joins relata of one cluster or one base: a citta's aggregates, the great elements *ekakalāpagatattā* (`abh04t.nrf.xml:2249`), mind and base at conception.
- *What physics adds.* A fibre F(e) over each element carrying a symmetric relation. That needs coincidence at a place, which every frame agrees on, not a simultaneity across distance.
- **AGREES in form** with relations physics keeps out of its causal order: constraints on a slice (∇·E = ρ; the ADM constraints ℋ ≈ 0, ℋᵢ ≈ 0) and local equations of state. **EXTENDS in name** (the texts count them as conditions). Measurement: none — philosophy.
- *Collision stated but not derived.*
  - With A24 (ADDED): if one citta's co-nascent matter arose more than c·τ ≲ 30 μm apart, co-nascence would need distant simultaneity. That would collide with special relativity's light cone (e.g. GW170817, |v_gw/c − 1| ≲ 10⁻¹⁵) and with nerve-conduction latency (Helmholtz 1850).
  - But the Vibhaṅga Mūlaṭīkā has joy pervade the body "by a series" (*pabandhena*, `abh02t.tik.xml:657`). No text in this aperture asserts the spread.

**D4 — Contiguity is a link only within a sort.**
- *Statement.* If A1, A3, A82, A112 and T3, the step from neither-perception to fruition is a link within the mental sort, while about seven days of material events lie between them.
- *Maths.* I_S(x,y) = {z ∈ S, same stream : x ≺ z ≺ y}, and x ⋖_S y ⟺ x ≺ y ∧ I_S = ∅. Then x ⋖_nāma y but ¬(x ⋖ y), with ℓ_nāma = 1 and ℓ_max ≈ 6×10⁵ s / 5.4×10⁻⁴⁴ s ≈ 10⁴⁹ (Brightwell and Gregory 1991).
- *The texts keep both counts:* the stream's own ("no interval") and the world's ("seven days").
- **AGREES** with run 1's survivor (kāla is designated per stream on its own count). **EXTENDS** causal-set theory, which has neither sorts nor links relative to a sort.
- *No collision.* A stopped series is not a running clock, so clock-universality tests are not engaged. Measurement: none — philosophy.

**D5 — What ends the gap.**
- *Statement.* If A112, A111 and T6, the fruition citta's proximity condition is about seven days old, but *when* it arises is set by events during the gap. None of those events can reach it as object, because the object condition always conditions a present citta (`abh03m7.mul.xml:53`).
- *The texts leave three options open (none forced):*
  - (a) A material carrier. But the call works by its *meaning*, and A61 says matter cognises nothing. Physics' own mechanism — unconscious semantic processing in sleep (Oswald, Taylor and Treisman 1960, *Brain* 83:440) — is excluded, because A111 cuts off even bhavaṅga.
  - (b) Natural decisive support from persons (*puggalopi*, `abh03m7.mul.xml:165`), with no carrier. Then "within earshot" is an unexplained constraint.
  - (c) The pre-cessation adverting took the future as object (§878 already requires knowing whether life will last seven days).
- *The case that separates them is (iv), with adverting.* Emergence lines up with a decision made in other streams, and no signal is sent.
  - Under (b) or (c), if that decision is fixed by a random draw *after* the meditator's last citta, any correlation is signalling. That **COLLIDES** with the no-signalling theorem and relativistic causal structure. This is a collision with a theory; no measurement on this population exists.
  - The texts' explanation ("so weighty is the command") is a further claim (2b). Under the same protocol it predicts emergence at the drawn time.
  - Case (iii) has a physical carrier (sound within earshot) and does not separate the texts from physics.
- **Measurement:** a randomized-call test with the meditator shielded from all signals. It needs a laboratory-verified seven-day cessation; none is cited in the corpus read.

**D6 — Future objects.**
- *Statement.* If A80, A88, A103 and T5, the object condition is the only one of the 24 whose condition may be absent or non-existent. Under presentism it is reference ("as a weak man holds a stick", `abh03a.att.xml:7685`), not production.
- Take object edges out and nothing runs backward in exercise time (with D2). So physics need add no backward causal edge — unless future-knowledge is right about events not fixed by present causes. Then it needs a future boundary condition that signals, which **COLLIDES** with no-signalling (a theory).
- *Measurements made so far.* Precognition tests on unselected participants, e.g. the Transparent Psi Project (Kekecs et al. 2023, *R. Soc. Open Sci.* 10:191375; figures from recall, fetch before citing), found nothing. That is not the population the texts claim.
- **EXTENDS.** Test: certified divine-eye attainers predicting a quantum-random outcome generated at least one cognitive process later, respecting the Kathāvatthu's "with interval".

**D7 — Kamma across time.**
- *Statement.* If A90 and A103, a ceased volition conditions a result, and the 24 give kamma-born matter no earlier material producer (`abh03m7.mul.xml:189`, `:201`, `:1145`).
- *Two options:*
  - (a) A trace in the present physical state — physics' default, which the texts neither assert nor deny.
  - (b) A dependence that skips the present, against screening-off (the Markov property of physical states).
- Classical sequential growth (Rideout and Sorkin 2000) already conditions each new element on its whole past: **AGREES in form**. It differs on existence: there the past stays, here it has ceased.
- Measurement: not realizable — philosophy.
