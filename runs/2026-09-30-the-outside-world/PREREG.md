## Predictions

1. Under A0 the external world's state, per kalāpa, is exhausted by the inseparable eight plus sound, so every physical property must be placed among earth, water, fire, air, colour, odour, taste and nutritive essence; each such placement (charge, spin, mass) is marked OURS, and the agent either names a collision with a precise measurement or labels the result philosophy.
2. Motion is treated as successive arising (A113 with A4, and A24 or a textual length): the agent derives a relation between a step length, the moment's duration and a maximum speed, and reports what the measured speed of light implies for the moment (expected: an upper bound on the moment's duration, compatible with A24's lower bound on the rate — EXTENDS, not COLLIDES), with every length or duration conversion labelled a bridge.
3. Light (āloka) is placed within the texts' own categories with a citation, or the agent flags that the table has no row for it; no third external kalāpa kind is invented.
