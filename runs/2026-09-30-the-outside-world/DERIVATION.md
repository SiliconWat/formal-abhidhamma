## 2. Derivations

**D0 — the texts' own configuration (guard 2e).** NOT A DERIVATION as a reading; its corollary is a derivation.
- The external world is a set Kᵉ of kalāpas.
- Each kalāpa has a kind ∈ {octad, nonad} (A66), carries E₈ (A15), and has a birth b ∈ ℤ counted in sub-moments, with life 51 = 17 × 3 (A4, R2).
- Each has a parent π(k) whose fire (hot *or* cold, R1) produced it during the parent's standing phase: b(πk)+1 ≤ b(k) ≤ b(πk)+49 (A64, R2, R3).
- Space is a pairwise delimiter with no position of its own (A5). Characteristics mark kalāpas and are not members (A66).
- Outside any stream, "17" is a **unit**, not anyone's clock.

**Corollary:** births strictly decrease along π. So a lineage that never passes through a body has no first member. A lineage that ends backward at a corpse (K) ends at kamma-born matter, which has no material parent. This is a refinement of the corpus's line "bodies and world-regions BEGIN": regions begin, but external lineages need not. Physics: **EXTENDS**; no measurement.

**D1 — degrees of freedom and Bell. COLLIDES on the relay branch.**
- Per kalāpa the texts allow ≤ 9 graded member intensities (R7: *omattādhimatta*), a kind, a birth and a parent. There is no carrier outside the members (A5, A66).
- The global state is separable by doctrine: R5's non-mixing.
- Values are definite. This is OURS, reading *sabhāvarūpa* (`abh07t:5817`) under A0.
- Add the relay option (R9 *yathā vā*, R6): a kalāpa depends only on its parent and its neighbours.
- Then, with free settings (OURS): |S| ≤ 2. Measured: **S = 2.42 ± 0.20, loophole-free (Hensen et al. 2015, Nature 526:682).**

The texts also hold the other option, so locality is not forced:
- R9's magnet, and R11's body crossing a far distance in a few citta-turns.
- On the magnet branch, Bell can be violated. Bancal et al. 2012 (Nat. Phys. 8:867) shows that any finite-speed hidden influence permits signalling, so the influence must be instantaneous. Yin et al. 2013 already bound such an influence at > 10⁴ c over 16 km (journal reference from knowledge).
- The magnet *as the texts state it* **signals**: a far sound shakes vessels and makes an echo at once. That collides with measured echo delays (reported-view run).
- A non-signalling magnet is OURS.

**Measurement:** the Bell tests already done.

**D2 — what "temperature-born" commits us to.**
- (a) **Parent rule.** No external kalāpa arises without a material parent (D0). This AGREES in form with local conservation (a continuity equation); the mapping is OURS. No measurement — **philosophy**.
- (b) **Thermal branch.** H-T (OURS): the timing of a change of kind in a successor is a function of the parent's fire intensity. β-decay makes new external matter, so its rate must vary with T. Measured: ¹⁹⁸Au half-life **2.684 ± 0.004 d at room temperature vs 2.687 ± 0.005 d at 12 K** (Goodwin et al. 2007, EPJ A). A power law r ∝ Tᵅ needs α ≲ 10⁻³ (our arithmetic, ~1σ). **COLLIDES.**
  - An ad hoc law with f(12 K) = f(293 K) clears this one measurement. It leaves the general null result for temperature dependence of decay (from knowledge, not checked here).
- (c) **Generic branch (G).** "Temperature" means the producing quality present in every kalāpa at every temperature. The texts' own "cold is fire" (R1, R3) and A15 support this. It clears (b) but empties "temperature" of thermal content: "temperature-born" becomes "born of prior matter". **EXTENDS**: physics has no single quality that originates every material change.
- (d) Temperature-born ≠ mind-independent. That is the texts' own position (R7, R8, R12): *origin* (samuṭṭhāna) is temperature, while *condition* (paccaya) may be mind or the kamma common to all beings. A0 is the choice that sets this aside. No measurement is specified at the texts' scale (cosmogony) — **philosophy**.

**D3 — what light is.**
- (i) Light, darkness, shadow and sunshine are visible form derived from the four great elements (R4, **Piṭaka**). Outside beings this is the colour member of temperature-born octads (A66). So **light is matter** and carries all eight (A15), odour and taste included. NOT A DERIVATION.
  - It AGREES in form with light carrying momentum and energy (radiation pressure, Lebedev 1901; Nichols & Hull 1903, from knowledge). Reading earth or air as momentum is OURS.
  - "Darkness is a positive colour": no measurement separates it from low radiance — **philosophy**.
- (ii) **Crossing beams. COLLIDES if colour takes a finite set of values.**
  - Assume: non-pervasion (R5: one kalāpa per site), one colour member per kalāpa (A15), and the relay.
  - Then two beams of colours c₁ ≠ c₂ crossing at a site leave from one value col(k×). By pigeonhole, |C×C| > |C| for finite C, so both cannot exit unaltered.
  - Measured: beams cross unaltered. Photon–photon scattering is seen only at the QED level: **13 candidate events over a background of 2.6 ± 0.7 (ATLAS 2017, Nat. Phys. 13:852)**.
  - Exits: (α) colour is a superposable field over direction × wavelength — **EXTENDS**; (β) a real scalar with a non-smooth ℝ ≅ ℝ² encoding — ad hoc; it clears the crossing but leaves (iii).
- (iii) **Interference** (Young 1803; single photons, Grangier–Roger–Aspect 1986, from knowledge) needs a **phase** that no text in this aperture names.
  - Relay branch: the colour member must be complex and linear.
  - At-a-distance branch (the Atthasālinī author's sight ruling): the conditioning law must compute path length mod λ, *and* it carries the reported-view run's delay collision (LLR 2.56 s).
  - **EXTENDS** on both branches.
- (iv) **Invariance.** A relay through octads has a rest frame: the medium's or the source's. Both are excluded (Michelson–Morley-type tests; source-independence of c, Alväger et al. 1964 — from knowledge). Only frame-relative counting survives, which needs the 17-count to dilate. That is the first run's muon collision, not re-derived here.
