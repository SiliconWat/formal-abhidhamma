**ADDED readings (not pre-registered; all VERIFIED)**

- **R-Kv** (Piṭaka). Kathāvatthu, `abh03m3.mul.xml:4505–4529`.
  - It refutes "one citta lasts a day" by quoting AN 1.48 and SN 12.61 (`:4517`, `:4521`).
  - At `:4529` it denies that "the very citta by which one sees with the eye is the one by which one hears". This is Piṭaka support for one object per citta across the senses, which is part of A1's ⏳.
- **R-snap** (commentarial). AN-a `s0401a.att.xml:1119` defines *accharāsaṅghātamatta* as "striking two fingers to make a sound".
- **R-mora** (grammatical treatise; tool layer "other"). `e0807n.nrf.xml:205`: the short-vowel time (one *mattā*) "is a finger-snap or an eye-blink".
- **R-uniform** (sub-commentarial). AN-ṭīkā `s0401t.tik.xml:1666`: *sabbassapi cittassa samānakhaṇattā*, "all cittas have the same moment".
  - Supporting line, `abh04t.nrf.xml:821`: by series, dhammas are short-lived or long-lived; by their own characteristic they are of one measure (*ekaparicchedā*).
- **R-serial** (sub-commentarial, an Abhidhamma anuṭīkā; tool layer "other"; I did not verify which work it is). `abh04t.nrf.xml:821`:
  - The citta rate is many koṭis per snap-strike, because the mind "turns quickly" (*lahuparivattitāya*, AN 1.48's word).
  - So when a pot and a dish are both in view, one is first discerned as "pot" by a mind-consciousness series. The other is discerned afterwards, with an eye-process in between.
  - People imagine the cognition is undivided only because they do not know the difference (*avisesavidutāya*).
- **R-firebrand.** A late sub-commentary on the Saṅgaha and Vibhāvinī (manual tier; tool layer "other"; I did not identify the author), `e0401n.nrf.xml:897`, `:1157`:
  - Eye-door processes arise at each place the whirled torch reaches.
  - Mind-door processes, "not releasing the past rūpas taken by the earlier eye-door processes, take them together as shape and series". The wheel appears because of this.
  - To know the wheel's shape, "many preceding-and-following process-series are required".
  - Same theme elsewhere: DN-ṭīkā `s0101t.tik.xml:1981` (proximity hides the previous citta's absence, "made plain by the firebrand wheel"); `abh04t.nrf.xml:745` (compactness of series, of group and of function).
- **R-khaṇa** (sub-commentarial, Visuddhimagga mahāṭīkā; the tool labels the layer "aṭṭhakathā"). `e0104n.att.xml:5581–5585`.
  - This is a reported view, so guard 2f applies. *Keci* say rise-and-fall of the immaterial aggregates is seen only by duration or series, never by moment. The author rejects this: "by their view there would be no seeing of rise-and-fall by moment".
  - He accepts the *apare*, who say that in moment-seeing one sees the arising and breaking of present dhammas: *taṃ yuttaṃ*.
  - His ruling: when knowledge is sharp and clear, "material and immaterial dhammas appear arising and breaking moment by moment" (*khaṇe khaṇe*).
- **R-javana** (manual tier). `e0401n.nrf.xml:909`: javana runs "at most seven times" (*sattakkhattu paramo*).

**Reported views and their authors' rulings (guard 2f)**

- **AN-a `s0401a.att.xml:1005`.**
  - Some teachers (*ekacce ācariyā*) say AN 1.48's *citta* means bhavaṅga. The author rejects this: any citta, "even eye-consciousness", is meant.
  - The same passage asks why the Buddha said a simile is not easy while the Milindapañha gives one. The ruling: no one asked him for a simile.
- **SN-ṭīkā `s0302t.tik.xml:1475` and AN-ṭīkā `s0401t.tik.xml:1666`** (both sub-commentarial).
  - The Milinda rice measure is textually unsettled: "*aḍḍhacuddasa* say some, *aḍḍhacatuttha* others … *vīmaṃsitabbaṃ*" (to be investigated). The ṭīkā's own ruling is undecided.

## 2. Derivations

**D0 — the texts' own numbers. NOT A DERIVATION (a reading).**

- koṭi = 10⁷ and satasahassa = 10⁵, so koṭisatasahassa = 10¹². *Anekāni* (plural, "many") gives N_snap ≥ 2·10¹². Reading the plural as ≥ 2 is ours.
- The snap's duration comes from the texts themselves:
  - R-snap: the strike that makes the sound, which S1 puts at about 7 ms.
  - R-mora: one short vowel, a snap or eye-blink, which S2 puts at up to about 0.4 s.
  - Window: τ_snap ∈ [7·10⁻³, 4·10⁻¹] s.
- ⚠️ For the main session: the deposited `abhidhamma-and-discrete-quantum-gravity.md:482` says "the text gives no duration" and assumes about 0.1 s. R-snap and R-mora are textual durations that sentence did not use. This is evidence, not a verdict.
- Milinda cross-check (ours, rough): one vāha taken as about 10³ kg of rice, one grain about 25 mg, 150 vāha → about 6·10⁹ grains. So the Milinda measure gives N_snap > ~10¹⁰. This is consistent with (weaker than) 10¹².

**D1 — feeling at the citta rate (internal consistency).**

- If A0′ (associated factors arise and cease with the citta) and A24(a), then feeling runs at the same rate. The Vibhaṅga-a figure for feeling is entailed by the SN-a figure for citta; it is not independent.
- ∀i: arise(vedanā_i) = arise(citta_i) ∧ cease(vedanā_i) = cease(citta_i) ⇒ N_vedanā(snap) = N_citta(snap) ≥ 2·10¹².
- **EXTENDS.** Affect science samples hedonic tone at 10⁻¹–10⁰ s and is silent below that.
- Measurement: none — philosophy. Nothing reports feeling at that grain.

**D2 — the length of one moment.**

- If A1 (moments do not overlap, so their durations add within a snap) and A24, then one moment lasts at most 0.2 ps across the whole window. On the strike reading alone it is at most 3.5 fs.
- N·τ_c ≤ τ_snap, N ≥ 2·10¹², τ_snap ≤ 0.4 s ⇒ τ_c ≤ 2·10⁻¹³ s (strike reading: τ_c ≤ 3.5·10⁻¹⁵ s). Hence f_c ≥ 5·10¹² Hz.
- The bound is one-sided: *anekāni* has no ceiling.
- **EXTENDS** for psychophysics, which measures nothing below about 10⁻³ s.
- **BRIDGE note (outside A0′):** no neural signalling event is faster than about 10⁻⁴ s. So if a citta has a physical correlate, it cannot be a spike, an oscillation or an ignition event. It would have to be molecular: vibrational modes at 10¹³–10¹⁴ Hz, or retinal isomerization at about 200 fs (RECALLED).
- Measurement: none — philosophy while the window is A0′.

**D3 — how many moments fit in one grain of experience.**

- If A1, A24 and the measured grain W (S3, S4, S7), then one reportable grain spans K = W/τ_c moments, with K ≥ 10¹⁰:
  - gap detection (2 ms): about 10¹⁰;
  - order threshold (20 ms): about 10¹¹;
  - access (300 ms): about 1.5·10¹².
- The texts derive the second, slow layer themselves (R-firebrand, R-serial; guard 2e): a mind-door synthesis over "many" processes, taking series, group and shape together.
- So the science of consciousness would need two scales:
  - a substrate of discrete, serial, single-object events at ≥ 5·10¹² Hz;
  - a synthesis layer whose grain is what psychophysics measures today.
- K = W/τ_c ≥ 2·10⁻³ / 2·10⁻¹³ = 10¹⁰. With A4 as a BRIDGE (a sense-door process of about 17 moments): ≥ 6·10⁸ processes per grain.
- **AGREES qualitatively** with discrete and postdictive perception (S7; perceptual-moment literature, RECALLED). **EXTENDS in magnitude.**
- Measurement: none for the substrate as such. Below W, seriality is invisible to report by construction. The one opening is D6.

**D4 — the knower always follows the known.**

- If A1 (including `s0203a:913`) and A0′ (one object per citta), then no citta knows itself. Any knowing of a moment i happens at some j > i. Every report is retrospective by at least one moment.
- ∀j: obj(j) = moment(i) ⇒ i < j.
- **AGREES** with constructive and postdictive timing (colour-phi and flash-lag class, RECALLED). Current theory also predicts this, so it does not distinguish.
- Measurement: none — philosophy at the moment scale.

**D5 — insight can only sample the stream.**

- If A1, `s0203a:913` (a moment is known by adverting to it with a later citta) and R-javana, then observing one moment costs one adverting citta plus up to 7 javanas taking that one object. At most about 1 moment in 8 can be an observed moment.
- Even the "moment by moment" of R-khaṇa is therefore a sub-sample, with a spacing of at least about 8τ_c ≤ 1.6 ps.
- Observed fraction ≤ 1/(1 + n_javana), with n_javana ≥ 1 and at most 7 (taking 7 gives 1/8). Resolution θ_ins ≈ 8τ_c ≤ 1.6·10⁻¹² s.
- **EXTENDS.** This is the texts' own configuration.

**D6 — the texts' own test.**

- If R-khaṇa (the author's ruling), D2 and D5, then a practitioner at udayabbaya-ñāṇa, the knowledge of rise and fall (by the texts' criterion), would see arising and breaking at resolution θ ≤ 1.6 ps. That practitioner should discriminate two flashes 5–20 ms apart: that gap is ≥ 2.5·10¹⁰ moments.
- R-khaṇa ∧ D2 ∧ D5 ⇒ θ_ins ≤ 1.6·10⁻¹² s ≪ 5·10⁻³ s.
- **COLLIDES** with S5. After three months at 16 h/day, successive-flash discrimination did not move, although single-flash detection did.
  - ⚠️ S5 did not assess the participants' ñāṇa stage. So S5 does not test R-khaṇa on its specified subjects. It is the nearest measurement already made, and it moved the wrong component.
  - The collision is with an appearance, so it survives the A0 → A0′ change of window (guard 2g).
- **BRIDGE collision:** if the "seen" passes through the eye, cone phototransduction integrates over tens of ms (RECALLED; Baylor–Nunn–Schnapf class). That is a physical limit, outside A0′.
- **Exits, named with what each leaves (guard 2g):**
  - (a) The *keci* reading (series only): clears D6, but contradicts the author's ruling at `e0104n:5581`.
  - (b) "khaṇa-seeing is of internal characteristics, not external succession": clears the flash form. It leaves D2 and D3 untouched, and turns D6 into philosophy unless an internal-timing task is specified (e.g. Libet-clock reports of noted events).
  - (c) "Order information finer than W is erased before any report": clears D6. It leaves the texts' claim that the discerning mind knows seriality (R-serial, *avisesavidutāya*) with no reportable consequence.
  - Each exit clears D6 alone. I did not show that no combination clears D6 while keeping R-khaṇa's author ruling; that claim is not made.
- **Distinguishing measurement:** pre-register temporal-order, two-flash and gap thresholds in practitioners whose stage is certified by their own teachers, blind to the psychophysics, against matched controls.
  - Texts read literally predict thresholds far below 1 ms (a step change, not a few percent).
  - Current theory predicts a few ms at best, and at most modest gains (compare S5 and Slagter et al. 2007 on the attentional blink, RECALLED).

**D7 — the moment is uniform, so felt duration varies in the series.**

- If R-uniform (every citta has the same moment) and A1, then no state changes τ_c. Fear, meditation and drugs can change only counts and synthesis. Distortions of felt duration must be series-level and must not bring finer resolution.
- τ_c = const ⇒ Δ(felt duration) = Δ(synthesis over counts), and θ is unchanged by state.
- **AGREES** with S6 (duration expanded, resolution unchanged). Not distinguishing: memory-based theories predict the same.

**D8 — seeing and hearing are never simultaneous.**

- If R-Kv (Piṭaka), A1 and R-serial, then any experience of a sight together with a sound needs at least two cittas at different indices. Felt simultaneity is a synthesis over an interval ≥ 2τ_c, and in practice over K (D3).
- obj(i) = x ≠ y = obj(j) ⇒ i ≠ j.
- **AGREES qualitatively** with a central serial bottleneck (S7, psychological refractory period) and with the global-workspace picture of one broadcast content at a time.
- **COLLIDES** if the citta is identified with a workspace ignition: S7 puts ignition at about 3 Hz, a gap of 10¹² from D2. The citta cannot be any currently measured correlate of a conscious content.
- The identification is ours, not the texts'. Without it, measurement: none — philosophy.

**D9 — AN 1.48's ordinal.**

- If A25 and A4, then rate(citta) = 17 × rate(rūpa), the fastest among dhammas.
- Under A0′ this is not testable by psychophysics. Because D2's bound is one-sided, no collision can be derived.
- **BRIDGE (outside the window):** if A0 were declared and rūpa were physical process, A25 would force τ_c below every physical timescale (attosecond electron dynamics). *Anekāni* allows this, so there is no collision, only an unbounded N.
- Measurement: none — philosophy.

**D10 — BRIDGE, ours, with a caution.**

- h/kT at body temperature is 0.155 ps. That falls inside D2's window [3.5 fs, 0.2 ps].
- A value landing inside a window two orders of magnitude wide is weak evidence. The STANDING caution about seductive coherence applies.
- Measurement: none clean — philosophy.

## 3. Where the axioms conflict

- **A4 is internally contested:** 17, 16⅓, and 16 at `s0101t:1981`. This changes the sense-process estimate by less than 10%; the orders of magnitude are unaffected.
- **A25 against the Milinda simile:** the commentary resolves it (AN-a `:1005`: no one asked). This is not a contradiction in the Piṭaka.
- **The two Milinda versions** (SN-a "exhausted"; AN-a "not a fraction of a fraction"), and the ṭīkā's unsettled reading of the measure: both are lower bounds and do not conflict with A24.
- **A1 against R-khaṇa:** a citta cannot know its own arising (`s0203a:913`), yet arising is "seen moment by moment". `s0203a:913` itself resolves this: the arising is known by adverting, with a later citta. The cost is D5: insight is a sub-sample.
- **Rate against simile (A24 against A25):** no contradiction. The Piṭaka gives an order ("faster than anything"); the commentary gives a number.

## 4. Formal statements for Lean

All models use a stream `ℕ → Moment` with duration τ : ℝ>0.

- **D1:** H: A0′ (vedanā_i shares arise and cease with citta_i), A24a (N_c ≥ 2·10¹²) ⊢ N_v = N_c ≥ 2·10¹². Model: vedanā = citta as the identity bijection on `Fin N`.
- **D2:** H: A1 (disjoint intervals: N·τ ≤ τ_snap), N ≥ 2·10¹², τ_snap ≤ 0.4 ⊢ τ ≤ 2·10⁻¹³. Model: τ = 10⁻¹³, τ_snap = 0.3, N = 3·10¹².
- **D3:** H: D2, W ≥ 2·10⁻³ ⊢ W/τ ≥ 10¹⁰. Model: same as D2, with W = 0.02.
- **D4:** H: A1 plus the self-knowledge clause ⊢ ∀j, obj(j) = i → i < j. Model: obj(j) = j − 1 for j ≥ 1.
- **D5:** H: D4; one object per process of 1 + n cittas, with 1 ≤ n ≤ 7 ⊢ observed density ≤ 1/2 in general, and ≤ 1/8 when n = 7; spacing ≥ (1 + n)τ. Model: period-8 stream with observed = {8k}.
- **D6:** H: R-khaṇa (θ ≤ spacing), D2, D5 ⊢ θ ≤ 1.6·10⁻¹². Model: θ = 8τ, τ = 10⁻¹³. **The comparison with S5 is NOT A DERIVATION.**
- **D7:** H: ∀i, τ_i = τ (R-uniform) ⊢ θ is independent of state; duration(state) = count(state)·τ. Model: constant τ, two states with different counts.
- **D8:** H: one object per citta, x ≠ y ⊢ i ≠ j. Model: obj = parity (x at even i, y at odd i).
- **D9:** H: A4 (τ_rūpa = 17τ) ⊢ τ < τ_rūpa. Model: τ = 1, τ_rūpa = 17.
- **Not derivations:** D0, and the S-comparisons in D2, D3, D6, D7 and D8. D10 is a numerical comparison only.

## 5. Theses for the refuter

1. D1: the Vibhaṅga-a feeling rate is entailed by the SN-a citta rate under association; it is not independent evidence.
2. D2: with the texts' own snap glosses (strike and mora), τ_c ≤ 0.2 ps, and ≤ 3.5 fs on the strike reading.
3. D3: one measured grain of experience holds at least 10¹⁰ cittas, and the texts themselves supply the slow synthesis layer (firebrand, pot/dish).
4. D4: every report of experience lags its object by at least one moment, and no citta knows itself.
5. D5: under the texts' own process structure, insight observes at most about 1 moment in 8.
6. D6: the Vism-mahāṭīkā's accepted khaṇa-seeing predicts that practitioners at the rise-and-fall stage discriminate successive flashes at sub-ms (literally ps) resolution. The one retreat measurement (S5) found no change, but did not certify stage.
7. D7: a uniform moment predicts duration distortions without resolution gains, which agrees with S6 and does not distinguish.
8. D8: seeing-with-hearing is serial (Kathāvatthu, Piṭaka), and the citta cannot be a workspace-ignition event (a gap of 10¹²).
9. D9: AN 1.48's ordinal plus A4 gives only a lower bound on the rate, so no collision is derivable under A0′.
10. D10 (BRIDGE): h/kT at body temperature falls inside the τ_c window, and that is not evidence.
