**Run `breath-in-the-unconscious` (a control): evidence only, no verdict**

**Short answer:** If A0, A47, A114 and the ṭīkās hold, deeply unconscious living humans do not breathe. Every textual voice found agrees on that. Under A0 this collides with measured spontaneous breathing in the vegetative state, in coma and under anaesthesia. The collision is at the level of what can be seen or felt, so it survives a move to A0′. A second collision comes from the texts' own account of how a body lives without breath: that account needs a heat source that uses no oxygen.

Instrument check: the `tipitaka` control passed (217 files; `anantarapaccayo` found at abh01a.att.xml:301; text 05d5d3c). I read ±15 lines around every line cited below.

## 1. Window and axioms

- **Window: A0** (rūpa-kalāpa are mind-independent physical reality). This is a CHOICE. Breath is mind-born matter, so the run also uses **the bridge** (mind timing matter).
- **Pre-registered (all VERIFIED in AXIOMS.md):**
  - A47: the stream continues unbroken for the life-span except in cessation (manual + Vibhāvinī).
  - A64: the four origins of matter; every bhavaṅga produces mind-born matter (manual).
  - A111: cessation (manual + Visuddhimagga + Piṭaka via MN 43).
  - A114: breath is mind-born only and absent in seven cases (commentarial + sub-commentarial + other).
- **ADDED, not pre-registered:**
  - A31 (perception, *saññā*, is in every citta) and A51 (within one existence, bhavaṅga is one type). Both manual; VERIFIED per AXIOMS.md, not re-read this run.
  - The **closure clause**: the seven cases are exhaustive. Vism-ṭīkā e0103n.att.xml:4829 (*yathāvuttasattaṭṭhānavinimuttassa … anuppajjanaṭṭhānassa abhāvato*) and the Sāratthadīpanī vin01t2.tik.xml:3281 (*pārisesato*). Sub-commentarial; VERIFIED.
  - **Breath defined** as nose-wind going in and out, and mind-born only (*assāsapassāsā cittasamuṭṭhānāva*). Vibhaṅga-a abh02a.att.xml:1077, commentarial; VERIFIED.
  - **Sleep is unmixed bhavaṅga**, with the limbs sinking (*abbokiṇṇe bhavaṅge … aṅgāni osīdanti*). Mūlaṭīkā abh01t.tik.xml:2173, sub-commentarial; VERIFIED.
  - **The swoon criterion does not use breath**: *visaññī* is "one from whom perception, marked by perceiving blue etc., has gone" (e0810n.nrf.xml:2097, other; a Pāli lexicon commentary that cites the Amarakośa). In use: the person "fell unconscious, regained perception, rose" (Channa, s0303a.att.xml:1231; the Bodhisatta, s0513a1.att.xml:2885; both commentarial). VERIFIED.
  - Vism VIII §238 (e0101n.mul.xml:6869, commentarial): the last breaths arise with the 16th citta before the death citta and cease with it. VERIFIED.

## 2. What the texts themselves say (READINGS, NOT A DERIVATION)

**R1 — which reading of *asaññībhūta*.** Dhammapāla's *vā* allows two: overcome by swoon, or born among the non-percipient. Our observation: the non-percipient beings live on a fine-material plane (A48). So the second reading repeats the list's own *rūpārūpabhavasamaṅgīnaṃ*, and only the swoon reading adds content.

**R2 — the voices on mechanism (guard 2f):**
- **Dhammapāla** (e0103n:4829; reported by name at vin01t2:3281):
  - in the womb, as under water, there is no breath because the passage is closed (*niruddhokāsatāya*);
  - the swooned are "like the dead, for lack of a producing citta" (*samuṭṭhāpakacittābhāvato*), and so are those in cessation;
  - fourth jhāna and the higher planes lack breath by nature.
  - A *keci* view on the same line (subtlety) is reported, not his own, and covers only the jhāna and the fine-material plane.
- **Mahāgaṇṭhipada**, reported with *pana* at vin01t2:3281: in the swoon, the occurrence of citta is too weak (*cittappavattiyā dubbalabhāvato*). Sāriputta gives no ruling of his own within ±15 lines.
- **Mūlaṭīkā** (abh01t:2173): a matter-producing citta always produces breath unless something obstructs it, "such as entering the womb" (*gabbhagamanādi*). The "such as" (*ādi*) leaves the list open.
- **Anuṭīkā** (abh04t.nrf.xml:2209) fills that open list with the Visuddhimagga's seven, *asaññībhūta* included.
- **Anudīpanī** (e0401n:1221) fills it with womb, under water, *bāḷhaṃ visaññībhūta* (the deeply unconscious), entering fourth jhāna, entering cessation, and the higher planes. It adds: "apart from these causes, breath and other mind-born matter do not differ."
  - This paragraph carries at least four voices: the Paramatthadīpanī's quoted words, the Mūlaṭīkā (named, *ānandācariya*), a quoted Vibhāvinī verse, and the Anudīpanī's own glosses. **The list is the Anudīpanī's own.**
- **Ledi, Paramatthadīpanī** (e0301n:4345–4349): he rejects the Mūlaṭīkā's principle (*taṃpi na yujjati*). He then uses the obstruction cases themselves as his proof that a citta which does not produce gross breath "still produces the other, subtle matter". So he keeps the outcome: no breath in those cases.
- **Abhidhammāvatāra-ṭīkā** (abh08t:5765): the fourth-jhāna citta produces matter but not breath. Cittas near death are extremely weak (*paridubbala*) and produce no matter at all.

**R3 — search for a text that holds the other option (guard 2g).** I searched:
- `asaññībhūt` (6 hits), `visaññībhūt` (18), `mucchāpare` (10), `mucchāya` (24);
- `samuṭṭhāpakacittābhāv` (2), `kāyasaṅkhārāsamuṭṭhāpan` (7);
- `assāsapassāsā pavattanti` (6), `assāsapassāsā natthi` (1).

No text found holds that the swooned breathe. The aperture is these spellings in the 217 CST files. Not run: Sanskrit sources (not in the CST), the bulk `mucchit-` hits (mostly metaphorical), and anything outside the CST.

## 3. Derivations

**D1 — the texts' answer.** If A47, A114 with its closure (ADDED) and R1, then for a living sense-sphere human h who is not in the womb, under water, in fourth jhāna or in cessation:

B(h,t) ⟺ ¬S(h,t)

where B is nose-wind going in and out, and S is swooned (perception of objects gone).
- So deeply unconscious people do **not** breathe, and sleepers **do** (sleep is not on the list, and the closure gives them breath). All five voices in R2 reach the same outcome.
- Corollary (with Vism VIII §238): a living person outside the other six cases who has stopped breathing must be swooned. So the swoon item has to absorb every respiratory arrest that is later resuscitated. The converse — sustained apnoea brings unconsciousness within minutes — **AGREES** with hypoxic physiology. The forward claim **COLLIDES** (D4).

**D2 — Dhammapāla read broadly contradicts A47 + A64.** Suppose:
- A47: a swooned person still has a stream; only cessation cuts it (A111);
- A64: every bhavaṅga produces matter;
- M1-broad: S ⟹ no matter-producing citta occurs.

Then ¬∃ h, t such that Living ∧ ¬Cessation ∧ S. Nobody ever swoons, which contradicts the texts' own reports (Channa, the Bodhisatta). The texts repair this in two ways: read M1 narrowly as "no *breath*-producing citta" (Ledi), or use weakness (Mahāgaṇṭhipada; abh08t). Those two repairs disagree on whether the swooned person's citta still produces subtle matter.
- **EXTENDS. Measurement: none — philosophy.** No physical marker separates mind-born matter from temperature-born matter of the same kinds (A65).

**D3 — sleep and swoon are the same at the level of citta.**
- By A31, perception is in every citta, so a living human cannot be *asaññī* in the sense of lacking the perception factor. The term must mean "no cognitive process", which is the lexicon's sense.
- With A47, A51 and the Mūlaṭīkā's reading of sleep, both dreamless sleep and swoon are bhavaṅga only, of one type. Yet B(sleep) holds and B(swoon) does not.
- So breath-production is not a function of citta type:

¬∃ g : Type → Bool, ∀c, BP(c) = g(type c)

The texts need a hidden variable: strength w (the weakness route, M2) or an obstruction o (M3):

BP(c) = MP(c) ∧ w(c) ≥ θ ∧ ¬o

- This blocks one exit. Some behaviourally unresponsive patients show command-following on fMRI (Monti et al., NEJM 362:579, 2010). That cannot be what separates breathers from non-breathers in the texts, because the texts' dividing line is not "are process cittas present?" — dreamless sleep has none, and it breathes.
- **EXTENDS.** Candidate test: any physical correlate proposed for w must put dreamless sleep above θ and every swoon below it. The texts name no such correlate.

**D4 — the test.** If A0 and D1, then

∀h, I: (∀t∈I, S(h,t)) ⟹ (∀t∈I, airflow(h,t) = 0)

**COLLIDES** with:
- **The vegetative state.** "Complete unawareness of the self and the environment", with brainstem autonomic function preserved and spontaneous breathing, for months to years (Multi-Society Task Force on PVS, NEJM 330:1499, 1994).
- **The brain-death protocol** (AAN/AAP/CNS/SCCM, Greer et al., *Neurology* 2023).
  - Coma, absent brainstem reflexes and apnoea are three separate criteria. Apnoea counts only if there is no breath at PaCO₂ ≥ 60 mmHg and ≥ 20 mmHg above baseline.
  - The test exists because comatose patients whose respiratory drive is intact do breathe.
  - Physiology places apnoea with death — the place of the texts' other item, *matānaṃ*.
- **Anaesthesia with spontaneous ventilation.** End-tidal CO₂ is recorded breath by breath in patients who do not respond to the surgical incision.

The texts supply their own detector: breath touching the nostril or upper lip (e0101n:6717). So this is a physical claim (guard 2d). It is at the level of appearance — air felt at the nostril, a mirror fogging, the chest rising — and **survives A0 → A0′**.

Not checked this run: breathing during vasovagal syncope, which is the Channa-type case.

**D5 — the texts' own account of breathless life (guard 2b).** The texts keep a breathless body alive through the life faculty and heat (A111 via MN 43, plus temperature-born matter, A64). Under A0, energy conservation gives:

C·Ṫ = P_aer + P_an + P_k − P_loss, with P_loss ≈ 80–100 W

- The aerobic yield is about 20 kJ per litre of O₂. The body's O₂ store, breathing air, is about 1.5–2 L. That store covers only about 6–8 minutes of heat.
- The anaerobic budget adds, by our own order-of-magnitude estimate, at most about an hour of heat, with lethal acidosis long before.
- Holding T = 37 °C through 7 days of cessation, or through a long breathless swoon, therefore needs P_k ≥ P_loss: a heat source that uses no oxygen. **That is what physics would have to add.**
- **COLLIDES** with:
  - energy conservation as checked by calorimetry: whole-body direct calorimetry (heat) and indirect calorimetry (O₂) agree to about one per cent (Atwater–Benedict respiration calorimeter, about 1905; recalled, not fetched);
  - haemoglobin desaturation within minutes of apnoea (Benumof et al., *Anesthesiology* 87:979, 1997; recalled, not fetched).
- **Distinguishing measurement:** simultaneous direct and indirect calorimetry, plus airflow, on a claimed cessation attainment or on any breathless swoon lasting more than 10 minutes. The texts predict heat output with zero O₂ uptake.
- Its appearance residue survives A0′: a breathless body growing cold to the touch.
- The cessation case reaches beyond the question asked; I flag it as such.

**D6 — the womb. AGREES (a comparison, NOT A DERIVATION).** Dhammapāla's mechanism for the womb is that the passage is closed while the cause still runs. Fetal breathing movements move fluid, not air (Dawes et al., *J. Physiol.* 220:119, 1972, in fetal lambs; recalled, not fetched).

## 4. What each addition clears and leaves, and the joint closure (guards 2a, 2g)

- **Add-1 (by definition: *asaññībhūta* := an apnoeic unconscious person).**
  - Clears D4.
  - It makes the Visuddhimagga's own inference circular: the meditator infers "your breaths exist" *from* "you are not *asaññībhūta*" (e0101n:6717).
  - It contradicts the texts' own breath-free criterion for swoon (e0810n:2097).
  - It leaves D5.
- **Add-O (our addition: the obstruction route read as upper-airway obstruction by lost pharyngeal tone).**
  - Clears D4 only for unmanaged supine airways.
  - It leaves D4 for every managed airway — recovery position, jaw thrust, laryngeal mask — where the still-unconscious breathe.
  - It predicts breathing effort during the swoon, which M1 and M2 deny, so it splits the texts' own mechanisms.
- **Add-D (the Anudīpanī's *bāḷhaṃ* taken as a depth threshold).**
  - With any behavioural threshold, such as the bottom of the Glasgow scale, D4 survives: the separate apnoea test is the measurement.
  - With the threshold set to "apnoea", it is Add-1.
- **Add-3 (the unconscious person's nose-wind is temperature-born, like the bodily winds of abh02a:1077).**
  - Clears D4 and D5 together, but only by deleting *cittasamuṭṭhānāva* on that same line.
  - "Breath is mind-born" then has no measurement left: philosophy.

**Joint closure:** no addition inside A0 clears D4 and D5 together while keeping all three of: (i) breath is nose-wind in and out, (ii) breath is mind-born only, (iii) the swooned, identified by loss of perception, stay on the list. Moving to A0′ is not an exit: D4, and the residue of D5, are appearances.

## 5. Where the axioms contradict each other

- A114 read as Dhammapāla broadly vs A47 + A64 (D2). The texts clear it themselves, by Ledi's narrow reading or by weakness.
- Ledi (subtle matter is still produced) vs the weakness route generalised from abh08t:5765 (extremely weak cittas produce none). No measurement separates them: philosophy.
- The closed list of seven (Vism-ṭīkā; *pārisesato*) vs the Mūlaṭīkā's open *ādi*.
  - Only the open list can cover conscious apnoea: voluntary breath-holding; the Bodhisatta's breathless (*appāṇaka*) jhāna before he fell (s0513a1:2885); high cervical cord injury.
  - The closed list cannot.
- "Like the dead" puts cessation (no citta, A111) and swoon (bhavaṅga present, A47) under one mechanism word.

## 6. Theses for the refuter

1. Every voice found (Dhammapāla, Mahāgaṇṭhipada, Mūlaṭīkā/Anuṭīkā/Anudīpanī, Ledi) holds the swooned breathless; none found holds the other option.
2. The non-percipient reading of *asaññībhūta* repeats *rūpārūpabhavasamaṅgīnaṃ*, so the swoon reading carries the item (our reading).
3. "No producing citta", read broadly, contradicts A47 + A64; read narrowly, it is Ledi's view.
4. *bāḷhaṃ visaññībhūta* is the Anudīpanī's own gloss on the Mūlaṭīkā's open *ādi*, not the Mūlaṭīkā's words.
5. Ledi rejects the Mūlaṭīkā's principle but uses the obstruction cases as proof, so he keeps the outcome.
6. By A31 and A51, sleep and swoon are the same at the level of citta, so the texts need a non-type variable (strength or obstruction).
7. Covert-awareness findings cannot rescue the texts, because dreamless sleep has no process cittas and breathes.
8. Under A0 the texts collide with the vegetative state, coma and anaesthesia with spontaneous ventilation; the collision is in appearances and survives A0′.
9. The brain-death protocol's separate apnoea test defeats any behavioural depth threshold.
10. Add-1 clears D4 by making the Visuddhimagga's own inference circular.
11. Add-3 clears D4 and D5 only by deleting *cittasamuṭṭhānāva*.
12. The texts' account of breathless life requires P_k ≈ 80–100 W with no O₂; calorimetry would separate it.
13. The womb item agrees with fetal breathing-movement physiology.
