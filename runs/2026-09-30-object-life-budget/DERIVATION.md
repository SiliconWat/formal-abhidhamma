## 1 · The texts' own configuration (NOT A DERIVATION)

The texts treat perception as **grasping-while-present**:
- Every eye-door citta takes a present object (A37, `:1045`).
- One process never mixes present and past objects (`:4885`).
- Matter can act only after at least one moment, at its standing phase (`:1237`; the lower limit at `:4845`).
- The life is fitted to the grasping, not the grasping to the life (R7).

**The reasons the texts give why late entry is shallow are both object-side:** a short remaining life means weakness (R1), and weak conditions mean late entry (R2). Subject-side causes appear only at swoon or death (fewer javanas, `:4873`) and when the stream is busy (R2 `:2773`).

**Answer in one line:** if the texts hold, perception is a fixed serial pipeline racing a deadline carried by the OBJECT. The object's own clock starts at its arising and sets how much of the pipeline runs.
