- **N1 (Piṭaka).** Lifts A114's cessation clause to Piṭaka.
  - In cessation the bodily formation has ceased, while life, heat and the faculties remain. The bodily formation is the in-and-out breath ("bodily, bound to the body"). Sources: SN 41.6 `s0304m.mul.xml:6875`, `:6887`; MN 43 `s0201m.mul.xml:6196`; MN 44 `:6372`.
  - On entering, breath ceases before perception and feeling. On emerging, the mental formation arises first and breath after it. "The mind developed beforehand leads him to that state" (`:6879`, `:6883`, `:6891`, `:6895`).
- **N2.** Life and heat depend on each other like flame and light. Piṭaka: MN 43 `s0201m.mul.xml:6176`. Sub-commentarial: the heat is the kamma-born fire element, kept "even a hundred years" by the material life faculty (MN-ṭīkā `s0201t.tik.xml:8853`). Commentarial: *āyu* is the material life faculty (MN-a `s0201a.att.xml:10605`).
- **N3 (commentarial + sub-commentarial).** The duration is set by a duration-delimiting citta before entry (MN-a `:10741`, `:10745`; MN-ṭīkā `:8977`).
  - **N3a.** Seven days is the cap "because a day's food keeps the clung-to body going seven days" (MN-ṭīkā `:8981`).
  - **N3b.** There is no death inside cessation, "since one dies with the last bhavaṅga" (Vism-ṭīkā `e0104n.att.xml:7213`).
- **N4 (commentarial).** How cessation ends (Vism XXIII §875–880, `e0102n.mul.xml:8445–8489`):
  - It ends at the delimited time.
  - If the meditator did not advert beforehand, it ends "at the mere utterance" of a summons from the Sangha or the Teacher, within earshot (`:8457`, `:8465`).
  - If he did advert, he emerges "at that time", before any messenger arrives (`:8453`, `:8461`).
  - Mahānāga, ringed by fire and a jeering crowd, emerged only at the delimited time (`:8445`).
- **N5. Two time-designations.**
  - **P1:** Buddhaghosa, "by the power of development there is no temporal immediacy there; we too say this" (Vism XVII §598 `e0102n.mul.xml:3701` = Paṭṭhāna-a `abh03a.att.xml:7697`). The Vism-ṭīkā names the teachers as Revata and glosses Buddhaghosa: "there IS time-separation, of seven days etc." (`e0104n.att.xml:3569`). The Vibhāvinī has neither-perception "ceased for seven days or so", with fruition arising "even after a time-interval" (`abh07t.nrf.xml:6653`).
  - **P2:** the Mūlaṭīkā (`abh03t.tik.xml:4309`, ≈ verbatim at Vism-ṭīkā `:3573`). "Since the usage 'time' rests only on the occurrence of dhammas, there is no time-interval": no immaterial dhamma intervenes, and "matter makes no interval, being another continuum". The Anuṭīkā (`abh05t.nrf.xml:4641`) raises both objections, "how then 'seven days … five hundred kappas'?" and "but the material continuum goes on between them". Its answer: mind and matter in one person are separate continua (*visuṃyeva*).
  - **2f, two voices in Dhammapāla's ṭīkā:** `:3569` glosses Buddhaghosa (P1); `:3573` is the shared Mūlaṭīkā account (P2). Which text borrowed from which is not settled here.
- **N6.** The Sammohavinodanī says "matter cannot go on leaving mind behind; the going of both is of one measure" (two walkers, `abh02a.att.xml:417`). It dates kamma-born matter at the three sub-moments of every citta (`:421`). This is A4's neighbourhood.
- **N7 (commentarial; the other option, 2g).** MN-a `:10605` reports *keci* ("some") holding cessation to be *sacittaka*, with mind. It rejects them by reductio (speech would then not have ceased either).

A7's own line lists *cittakālo rūpakālo*, mind-time and matter-time, as separate designations (`abh01a.att.xml:3721`).

### 2. The texts' own configuration (2e) — NOT A DERIVATION

Breath ceases already at the fourth jhāna (MN-a `:10745`). Two neither-perception javanas follow, then the citta-stream is cut off (A111).

During the gap:
- Kamma-, temperature- and nutriment-born matter go on (A64). Mind-born matter, including breath, stops (A64 *jāyantameva*; A114).
- The life faculty keeps the kamma-born heat (N2). The faculties are clearer than usual, "a mirror in a bag" (MN-a `:10609`).
- No death can occur (N3b).

Emergence comes at the delimited time (N3), or at a summons (N4). It is one fruition citta and then bhavaṅga (A111), and only after that breath (N1).

Order: the Paṭṭhāna makes neither-perception the proximity condition of fruition (`abh03m10.mul.xml:3461`, verified).

Time: P1 and P2 are both held. The configuration is **two clocks in one person**: mind-time gapless (P2) and matter-time or day-time about 7 days (P1). Neither is forced; each has its holder.

Guard: the gap is not nibbāna, and nothing "remains" in it. Cessation is neither conditioned nor unconditioned (A111). Nibbāna enters only as fruition's object on emerging.

### 3. Derivations

**D1. Order without a gap, time with one (bridge).**
- **Statement.** If A3/A112 (σ(m₀) = m₁), A111 (no citta between them), A64/A67 (body matter arises throughout) and A7 with both designations, then:
  - no moment of the stream lies between m₀ and m₁ in either clock;
  - matter-time records Δ ≈ 7 d across that pair;
  - so the citta count is not a clock of the body's time.
- **Maths.** τ_c(σm) = τ_c(m) + 1 and τ_r(m₁) − τ_r(m₀) = Δ ≫ 1. Then ∄x ∈ Mₛ with τ_r(m₀) < τ_r(x) < τ_r(m₁), and τ_r ∘ τ_c⁻¹ is not affine.
- **Consequences.**
  - The memory ruling "Tₛ ≅ (Mₛ, ≺)" holds only for P2's τ_c.
  - Run 1's survivor ("for embodied streams the count acts as a clock along one worldline") is **narrowed**: across cessation the count stops while the worldline runs.
  - `Axioms.lean` `Stream.tick_succ` (+1) formalises P2 only and cannot express Buddhaghosa's interval.
- **AGREES.** A halted process is not a clock. Proper time is fixed along the body's worldline, and the body's own matter reads about 7 d. As a lens only, P2 is Page–Wootters relational time (PRD 27:2885, 1983; from knowledge, not fetched) with a clock subsystem that does not change over an interval, so the interval gets one reading.
- **Measurement:** none separates P1 from P2. **Philosophy.**
- **Formal.**
  - Hypotheses: A3, A111, A64, A7(P1+P2), plus co-monotonicity (τ_c a < τ_c b ↔ τ_r a < τ_r b).
  - Conclusion: as above.
  - Model: Moment = ℤ; σk = k+1; τ_c = id; τ_r k = k for k ≤ 0, k + Δ − 1 for k ≥ 1.
  - Break: impose τ_r(σm) = τ_r m + 1; this contradicts Δ > 1.

**D2. Matter dated on mind is undefined inside cessation (internal).**
- **Statement.** If N6 (kamma-born matter arises only at the stream's own citta sub-moments), A4 (17), A111 (a gap of Δ > 17 units) and N1/A65 (the life faculty and sense-faculties, which are kamma-born, persist through the gap), then contradiction.
- **Proof sketch.** Take t = a + 17. Any kalāpa present at t was born in (a, t], which lies inside the gap where no citta exists.
- **Consequence.** Within a LIVING human, kamma-born matter must be dated in mind-moment UNITS (Vibhāvinī `abh07t.nrf.xml:4849`, *ekacittassa khaṇaṃ viya khaṇaṃ*). The two-walkers rule (N6 `:417`) fails for Δ. This adds a third case to the paper's two-case split (§5.3: a stream exists / none exists): a stream exists, but its mind is cut off.
- **Other option, 2g.** I searched for a text dating kamma-born matter in cessation otherwise: *nirodhasamāpannassa* (7 hits), *antonirodh* (19). None found. The unit dating is the only one found; it is not shown to be the only one possible.
- **Physics:** AGREES trivially (physics dates by an external unit). **Philosophy.**
- **Formal.**
  - Hypotheses: N6, A4, A111, N1 + gap length > 17.
  - Conclusion: ⊥.
  - Model of the hypotheses without N6: kalāpas born at every integer.
  - Break: gap ≤ 17 is consistent.

**D3. Breathless, warm, alive (physiology, A0). COLLIDES.**
- **Tier.** The state is Piṭaka (N1). The duration is at least a night in the Piṭaka itself: MN 50, Sañjīva emerged "after that night" (*tassā rattiyā accayena*), and herders had judged him dead and burned him (`s0201m.mul.xml:6924`). The commentaries give up to 7 d.
- **Arithmetic.** O₂ store ≈ 1.5–2 L ÷ resting V̇O₂ ≈ 0.25 L/min ⇒ about 6–8 min (textbook values, not fetched).
- **Measured ceilings:**
  - AIDA static apnoea 11 min 35 s (Mifsud 2009).
  - 29 min 03 s after 10 min of breathing pure O₂ (Maričić, Guinness).
  - Naked mole-rat at 0% O₂: 18 min, and none recovered from 30 (Park et al. 2017, *Science* 356:307).
- **Size of the gap.** One night ≈ 600 min ⇒ about 20× the human ceiling. 7 d = 10,080 min ⇒ about 350×.
- **The texts' own explanations, added and tested (2b):**
  - **Kamma-born heat with no O₂ uptake (N2).** Under A0 this is about 80 W × 7 d ≈ 48 MJ from no oxidised fuel. It collides with Atwater & Benedict (1899): heat measured directly matched heat computed from oxygen use within 0.2% (11.37 vs 11.39 MJ/day, 40 days).
  - **No death without a death-citta (N3b).** This changes the word "death", not the tissue. MN 43's "faculties clear" is a claim about the tissue.
  - **The food cap (N3a).** It collides with multi-week fasting survival, e.g. a supervised 382-day fast (Stewart & Fleming 1973; from knowledge, not fetched). Oxygen, not food, binds, and about three orders of magnitude sooner.
- **Ad hoc additions, priced (2g):**
  - (a) **Suspend metabolism.** Clears the O₂ need. Leaves "heat not subsided" (the body cools toward ambient), and at body temperature it collides with the anoxia ceilings above.
  - (b) **A substrate-free kamma energy term powering cells and heat.** Clears all four conditions together, but is itself the collision with calorimetry and mass balance (no CO₂ output, no substrate loss).
  - **Joint closure:** no addition inside A0 clears no-breath + heat + life with clear faculties + at least a night together.
- **Window (2g).** The collision survives A0 → A0′: it is about appearances in other streams (MN 50's herders; MN 43 offers the corpse comparison as something observable).
- **Measured so far.** A self-described attainer (Armstrong) kept breathing during events of 10–20 s. His longest stay ("six days") is self-reported (Psyche essay; Laukkonen et al. 2023). By SN 41.6's own mark those events are not textual cessation. That is a further claim, testable by the same mark. No measurement yet touches the textual state.
- **Distinguishing measurement.** Capnography, SpO₂, core temperature, direct + indirect calorimetry and body mass, for more than 30 min, on a practitioner following the texts' entry sequence. Physics: ventilation continues, or the subject dies. Texts: zero ventilation, a warm core, recovery. A cheaper test of the same mechanism is the fourth jhāna (MN-a `:10745`; Vism VIII `e0101n.mul.xml:6717`).
- **Formal.** NOT A DERIVATION (comparison with measurements). Only the arithmetic can be Lean-checked: 600/29 > 20; 10080/29 > 340; 80·604800 ≈ 4.8·10⁷ J.

**D4. Scheduled emergence needs a carrier (bridge).**
- **Statement.** If A111, N3 (a pre-entry citta fixes when the next one arises), A0 and **P_loc** (ADDED as a physics premise: the state at t+1 is a function of the state at t), then a body that is stationary through the gap cannot emerge on schedule.
- **Maths.** With dynamics f and emergence predicate E: if f(s₀) = s₀, then E(fᵏ s₀) = E(s₀) for every k. So emergence happens at step 0 or never, which contradicts D ≥ 1.
- **Consequence.** Under P_loc the delimitation must be encoded in the successive states of the body's matter. Each state arises anew; nothing persists.
- **AGREES in kind.** ACTH rises in anticipation before a pre-announced early waking (Born et al. 1999, *Nature* 397:29). **EXTENDS in degree** (days, and no citta at all).
- **The texts' own account.** The Mūlaṭīkā says dhammas "have no operation" (`:4309`) and that the "capacity" is only the mode in which, once it has ceased, such dhammas arise. That is a regularity with no carrier. Taken alone it **COLLIDES** with time-locality, a feature of theory, not a measurement.
- **Distinguishing measurement.** Is the body physiologically stationary through the gap, with emergence still on schedule?
- **Formal.**
  - Hypotheses: A111, N3, P_loc, f s₀ = s₀, D ≥ 1.
  - Conclusion: ⊥.
  - Model: state = a counter, f decrements, E = (s = 0).
  - Break: let E depend on k directly (time-nonlocal); a schedule then succeeds with a stationary state.

**D5. The summons has no bearer (bridge). EXTENDS, with the texts' own test.**
- **Statement.** If A61 (matter cognises nothing), A111 (no citta) and N4 (a summons ends cessation; fire and crowd noise do not), then the person's dhammas hold nothing that discriminates a summons from other sounds. The commentary's mirror-in-a-bag simile leaves even the ear no textual channel.
- **The texts' reason, as a further claim (2b).** "Weighty is the Sangha's command" (*garukā hi saṅghassa āṇā*, `:8457`).
- **Tests:**
  - Identical words from an authorised messenger and from an unauthorised speaker, blinded. Texts: only the authorised one ends cessation. Physics: no dependence on authority not carried by the signal.
  - The adverting clause: randomised, sealed Sangha decisions with no sensory channel. Texts: emergence is timed to the decision. Physics: no correlation.
- **Formal.** A contradiction search (the discriminator ∉ the person's dhammas). A model with an external "authority" variable satisfies it. Otherwise NOT A DERIVATION (a test design).
