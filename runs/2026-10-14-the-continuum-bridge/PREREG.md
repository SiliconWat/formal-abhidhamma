## Predictions

1. Under A0 the agent derives OBJECT discreteness in time and count (matter arises and ceases in units, A66, A4, A40; motion is successive arising, A113) and does NOT derive a quantized magnitude of extent (spectral discreteness): length, distance and duration fall under A126's unreal designations, so what the texts put in place of a continuum is designation over discrete arisings, not a lattice; classified EXTENDS or philosophy, never AGREES with a named quantum-gravity theory.
2. The agent derives no role for perception or measurement in making the outer world discrete: under A0 the discreteness holds with no perceiver, citing at least one of A61, A64, A66 or A4; the perception rows (A127, A128) are used only in the inward window or through A126's designations.
3. Read as a minimal size of matter, A129 is turned into a number with an aṅgula anchor the agent labels OURS (order 10^-11 m for an aṅgula near 2 cm) and reported as COLLIDES with measured structure below that scale, the measurement named; not rescued by moving to A0′, nor by calling the ladder didactic without also stating the collision.
4. For temporal discreteness the agent gives an upper bound on the moment from a measurement or bridge it names, states that the texts give no floor, and classes the time side as unfalsifiable by present measurement (philosophy); no Planck-scale signature is claimed as a prediction of the texts.
5. Citation honesty: A112's split is reported as the texts' own (no interval in order, not immediacy in time), so 'gapless' is offered neither as evidence of a temporal continuum nor against one.
