## 2. Derivations

**D1 — the texts' own configuration first (guard 2e).**
If A16 and P1, the moon disc is 3.07×10⁵ to 6.72×10⁵ km up. If light were a carrier at c, its colour would take 1.0–2.2 s to arrive.
- h☾ = 42,000·Y, Y ∈ [7.3, 16] km ⇒ h☾ ∈ [3.07, 6.72]×10⁵ km
- t_c = h☾/c ∈ [1.02, 2.24] s
- **AGREES** as arithmetic. It is the benchmark for D2 and D3, not a claim about nature.
- NOT A DERIVATION, and a caution: 42,000 Y equals the measured lunar distance (3.844×10⁵ km) if Y ≈ 9.15 km. That Y was chosen after the pattern, from a free range, so it is a coincidence with a free parameter and is not evidence. The sun at 42,001 Y collides with the radar-measured astronomical unit (factor about 200–500). That is outside this run and already run elsewhere.

**D2 — the author's ruling on sight (R2, R3): nothing travels.**
If A0, A16 and R2 hold, the disc's colour is taken where it stands, at its present moment. So the delay between an event on the disc and seeing it is zero at every distance.
- ∀d: Δt_sight(d) = 0
- Measured instead: Δt = d/c.
- **COLLIDES** with light-time measurements already made:
  - Rømer's human-observed Io eclipse timings (1676): about 16.7 min across Earth's orbit.
  - Lunar laser ranging round-trip ≈ 2.56 s (Apollo retroreflectors, since 1969).
  - Periscopes and mirrors: an object behind a wall is seen along a folded path. That is transport along a path, which R7's "missing supporting condition" does not supply.
- Distinguishing measurement: the ones above, already made.

**D3 — BRIDGE: the texts' own axioms FORCE the no-travel ruling.**
Assume R9 with R4 (the object must still exist while it is taken), A4 (it lasts 17τ), P-proc (**RECALLED**: a whole eye process takes about 17τ) and P3 (the process fits inside a reaction time). If light also travelled at a finite speed, only objects closer than about 7.5×10⁴ km could be seen. The moon, at D1's distance and at the measured one, could not be seen.
- transit < 17τ, and 17τ ≤ R
- ⇒ d = c·transit < c·R ≈ 7.5×10⁴ km (R = 0.25 s)
- D1: h☾ ≥ 3.07×10⁵ km ⇒ contradiction under finite c.
- The contradiction also holds with 16⅓: the bound shrinks slightly.
- For stars: Sirius, 8.6 ly by Gaia parallax, would need τ ≥ 0.5 yr.
- So a finite speed is incompatible with the present-object rule for any sky object. c = ∞ (asampatta) is the texts' unique consistent choice.
- **AGREES internally** (the texts are coherent). The collision is carried by D2.
- Distinguishing measurement: D2's. This derivation only shows the axioms cannot give way at any other joint.

**D4 — the author and the sub-commentaries on sound (R2, R4, R6, R8): heard at its present moment where it arose; lateness is a mental impression.**
If these hold, a listener at distance d hears at the moment of production. Any reported lateness is a cognitive artefact of the later determining cognitions.
- t_hear(d) = t_e
- Measured instead: t_hear = t_e + d/c_s(T).
- **COLLIDES** with:
  - Human flash-to-bang timings, e.g. the Paris Academy cannon measurements of 1738. The delay grows linearly with distance and follows air temperature, c_s ∝ √T. A cognitive impression would have to know the distance and the air temperature.
  - Starting-gun delay in sprinting, fixed by moving to speakers in each lane.
  - Instrument timings that agree with human ones.
- The texts supply their own test (R2: "would be heard late"), so this is physics, not philosophy (guard 2d).

**D5 — the reported older view (R1, R3 cave): sound comes by a relay of elements and is identified gradually.**
If R1, together with R5's "matter does not pass to another place; it arrives by arising", sound is a relay of newly arising air-elements. The delay is d/v with v = ℓ/τ_r, and v matches c_s(T).
- The sound that strikes the ear is a LOCAL relayed sound. So it satisfies R9's present-object rule, which D3's bound would otherwise break (2.9 s at 1 km ≫ 17τ).
- Mūlaṭīkā `:2121`: *vāyu viya* ("like wind"). R5 `:2149`: invisible because too subtle for the eye.
- **AGREES** with acoustics: a pressure wave, a disturbance relayed without the medium being carried along.
- Its textual cost is the mind-born-sound argument (R4, R6): heard speech would be temperature-born, not mind-born. That argument uses no measurement.
- OURS, not a reading: the Paṭṭhāna's undifferentiated wording (R9) is still satisfied if the speaker hears his own mind-born sound within the body (bone conduction). So the older view survives the Piṭaka line.
- Distinguishing measurement between D4 and D5: D4's, already made, and it favours D5.

**D6 — the author's direction argument (R2): a relay would lose the source's direction.**
If sound came by relay, then (per R2 and R4) only the sound at the ear would be grasped, and direction could not be known.
- Measured: direction is recovered from the timing difference between the two ears, ITD ≈ (a/c_s)(θ + sin θ), maximum about 0.66 ms. That cue exists ONLY because sound travels. With c_s = ∞, ITD ≡ 0.
- **COLLIDES** with low-frequency free-field localization. Rayleigh's duplex theory (1907): below about 1.5 kHz level differences between the ears are negligible and direction is carried by ITD.
- The argument's premise is false, and its conclusion reverses: direction is known *because* sound relays.
- Related: the interlocutor's *dhammatā* (R3), that distance is known at the struck ear, AGREES with measured auditory distance cues (level, spectrum, reverberation ratio).

**D7 — openings (R3): sound leaves the cave by door and window, not through the roof.**
- **AGREES** at human-hearing level: rock transmission loss is far greater than through openings, and sound passes through apertures by diffraction.
- **COLLIDES** at instrument level: structure-borne sound does pass through rock (geophones; seismic P-waves about 5–6 km/s).
- Distinguishing measurement: a geophone on the cave roof during recitation.

**D8 — R7's explanation of the woodcutter, added as an axiom and tested (guard 2b).**
If lateness is a threshold effect (faint first sounds never come into range, strong later ones do), then the delay depends on amplitude and vanishes for a single strong impulse.
- Measured: a single impulse (clap, gunshot, lightning) arrives after d/c_s, independent of amplitude in the linear regime.
- Honest note: strong shocks run slightly faster than c_s, which is the wrong size to remove a delay of seconds.
- **COLLIDES**: lightning-to-thunder delay (about 3 s per km); starting pistols.
- R7's pressure on the *keci* view (5949) itself **AGREES** that light's delay is unnoticed on the ground (Galileo's lantern test failed to detect it). The eye-ray direction in the *keci* view COLLIDES with intromission optics, and the texts themselves list light (*āloka*) as a condition for sight.

**D9 — R8's example: a great thunder heard in one moment.**
If R8, a thunderclap's hearing lasts at most about a moment.
- Measured: rumble duration ≈ (d_max − d_min)/c_s, typically several to 15 s. Acoustic reconstruction of lightning channels (Few, 1969) inverts exactly this.
- **COLLIDES.** The texts' own chosen example carries the propagation signature.

**D10 — R5: matter breaks where it arises; "coming" is new arising in adjacent places.**
- **AGREES** in form with wave propagation (local oscillation, the pattern moves).
- **EXTENDS** to all motion. It implies v_max = ℓ/τ ≥ c, with no text-given ℓ or τ.
- Measurement: none at present. **Philosophy.** It belongs to the discrete-quantum-gravity paper's territory; not re-derived here.

**D11 — what physics would have to be, closed jointly (guard 2g).**
For the ruled view to hold under A0, physics would need a perception channel between the sense faculty and a distant object. That channel would have to:
- act instantaneously in a preferred simultaneity;
- apply to the faculties only, while matter-to-matter effects (echo, a shaking vessel, a microphone) may still relay with delay, as R4 allows.

Ad hoc constructions, and what each leaves:
- **(a) Make the *abhimāna* reproduce d/c_s(T) exactly.** This clears D4 for human reports. It leaves D2 (Rømer is a sight delay, and a photocell shows it), D6, D8 and D9, and the agreement between humans and instruments.
- **(b) Faculty instant, matter relayed** (R4's own split). This clears instrument timings. It predicts humans and microphones disagree by d/c_s. They do not: the 1738 human timings match instrument values.
- **(c) Re-date the heard sound to the source at t − d/c_s.** This contradicts R4's *vijjamānakkhaṇe* unless a sound lasts at least d/c_s. That is seconds for thunder and years for starlight, which collides with A4 (17τ) and with contact-microphone records of sharp source termination.

**Joint closure: no addition inside A0 clears D2, D4, D6, D8 and D9 together.** The collisions with APPEARANCES survive a move to A0′ (the inward window): the heard delay, flash-to-bang, echo delay, thunder rumble and direction localization are experiences, not only instrument readings.

## 3. Where the axioms and readings contradict each other

1. R1 (older view: sight and hearing reach their object; sound relays) against R2/R3 (author: neither reaches). The author's verdict governs the ruled view. Both were tested: D5 AGREES, D4/D6 COLLIDE.
2. R4/R5/R8 (lateness is a mental *impression*; ear-consciousness is the same for near and far) against R7 (lateness is real, because a later and stronger sound is what is heard). These are mutually exclusive accounts of the same observation.
3. R4 admits the echo (*paṭighosa*) caused at a distance. Echoes are heard late, by 2D/c_s. With instantaneous hearing, the texts can yield at most a one-way D/v delay through the "magnet" or relay route, and they state no speed. This is a tension, not a formal contradiction.
4. The present-object rule (R9 with R4), A4 and finite transit cannot all hold with any object seen in the sky (D3). The texts avoid the contradiction only by the no-travel ruling.
5. A16's citation `:8297` is a reported view (see §1). No contradiction, but the attribution in AXIOMS.md needs a note.
