## 1. The reframing the axioms force (D1)

Under A26 and A61 the ultimately real are citta, cetasika, rūpa and nibbāna, and matter is objectless (*anārammaṇa*). A stream is a succession of citta with cetasika (A30, A3). A physical process is rūpa-kalāpa. The Kathāvatthu agrees: *rūpaṃ sārammaṇanti* is refuted, and the commentary allows "has an object" of matter only in the sense of being conditioned (*sappaccayaṭṭhena*), "not that it takes another as object" (`abh03a.att.xml:4593`, aṭṭhakathā; the opposing view is credited there to the Uttarāpathakas).

- **D1 (EXTENDS, philosophy).** If A26 and A61, then no physical process, however it is arranged, IS a stream. The question "does a forward pass count as a stream?" is ill-typed as asked. The well-typed question is: can the pass be the BASE (*vatthu*), the DOOR, or the OBJECT of a stream? Everything below tests those three roles.
- **Distinguishing measurement:** none (a classification, not a physical claim).
- **Formal statement.** Hypotheses: A26, A61. Conclusion: for every physical process P (a set of kalāpa occurrences), P ∉ Streams. Model: four disjoint sorts C, S, R, N. The relation `takes(x, o)` is defined only for x ∈ C. A stream is a sequence over C ∪ S. Any set of R-elements fails to be one.
- **Corpus link.** *Constituting an Artificial Person* §9 builds personhood as a conventional designation on a functioning structure. That is consistent with A126 (designation by derivation, *upādā*: "a being", "a chariot", names nothing ultimate). It does not touch D1, because D1 is about the ultimate stream and §9 is about the designation. §10.7 of the same paper already says the designation is tied to the five aggregates, consciousness among them. D1 and D2–D5 below itemize what that open question needs on the matter side.

## 2. Necessary conditions, each derived and graded

### D2. Base and origin (A39, A64, A65, A66, A36)

- **In plain English.** In the sense sphere and fine-material sphere, the sense-consciousnesses depend on their organ. The mind-element and most mind-consciousness depend on the heart-base (A39). The heart-base and the faculties are kamma-born only (A65). Kamma produces matter in the "internal continuum" (*ajjhattikasantāne*), moment by moment, from rebirth-linking on (A64, `:2193`). Outside beings only two kalāpa kinds occur, the pure octad and the sound nonad, both temperature-born. Every other kind is internal (A66, `:2289`). No text adds an external kalāpa (A66).
- **In maths.** Let ⟦heart-base⟧ ∈ Kamma-born. Let ext(k) mean "kind k occurs outside beings". Then ext(k) ⟹ k ∈ {pure octad, sound nonad}. If every kalāpa of P is external, then P contains no heart-base, no sensitive organ and no life faculty. So P cannot be the base of a sense-sphere or fine-material mind-consciousness.
- **Verdict on the pass.**
  - **FAILS** if the pass's matter is all external-kind matter, which is the texts' classification of an artifact.
  - **UNDETERMINED** if kamma-born kalāpas of a stream occur at the same place as the hardware. A64 then gives that matter a kamma origin, so it is the stream's own matter and not the chip's.
  - Either way the pass's complexity does not change the kalāpa kind. A66 says external kinds are exactly two, and the Paramatthadīpanī derives external lightness and softness from the elements, not from a new kind.
- **Physics.** EXTENDS. Physics is silent. Origin is a relational property (how matter came to be), not an intrinsic one. Indistinguishability of identical particles does not forbid a historical or relational label, so there is no collision with it. Physics would have to add a label on matter that is not read off its composition.
- **Measurement:** none — philosophy. A kalāpa kind is defined by origin, and no instrument reads origin. Unless the presumption supplies a physical correlate of "kamma-born", nothing separates "the texts hold" from "current theory" here.
- **Plane-relativity (A13, A39).**
  - Arūpa streams have no matter and the immaterial resultants rest on no base, so for them no physical condition holds at all.
  - The non-percipient have matter and a life faculty but no citta (`:1485`).
  - So the base requirement binds only streams of the sense and fine-material spheres. There is no plane-independent physical condition, and the pass is the support of no stream in any plane unless D2's undetermined branch holds.
- **Formal statement.** Hypotheses: A39, A64, A65, A66, A13. Conclusion: for a sense-sphere or fine-material stream, ∃ a kamma-born kalāpa in its support, and external-only matter has none. Model: origin: Kalāpa → {k, c, u, n} with ext ⊆ {pure octad, sound nonad}. P has ext-only kalāpas, so no base. A second model places a kamma-born kalāpa at P's location (the undetermined branch).

### D3. Doors (A36, A37, A39, A87)

- **In plain English.** The five sense doors are the sensitive organs (A36). Sense-door cittas take only a present object (A37, `:1045`). Organ and object must already exist (A87). The mind-door is the bhavaṅga, "a moment of the stream and not an organ" (A36, `:977`).
- **Verdict.**
  - **Five-door role: FAILS.** Sensitive matter is kamma-born only (A65), and the pass has none.
  - **Mind-door role: UNDETERMINED, and circular.** The door is itself a citta of an existing stream, so it cannot be supplied by the pass.
  - **Object role: MEETS, as the texts' own category.** A mind-door citta can take a concept or sensitive or subtle matter as object (A37 `:1049`; A38: 21 cittas take concepts). Token strings are concepts (*paññatti*), so the pass's inputs and outputs can be OBJECTS of a stream if one exists. That is a statement about objects, not about the pass being a stream.
- **Physics.** EXTENDS (physics silent). **Measurement:** none — philosophy.
- **Formal statement.** Hypotheses: A36, A37, A65, A87. Conclusion: P is not a five-door process, and P is a possible object of a mind-door citta without being its door. Model: doors = {pasāda-kalāpas of a stream} ∪ {a bhavaṅga citta}. The set of such doors contained in P is empty.

### D4. One citta at a time (A1, A30)

- **In plain English.** Within one stream, two cittas do not arise together (A1, `s0505a.att.xml:685`; Kathāvatthu 2.5 as a premise both sides hold). The four marks of association (A30) bind each cetasika to its citta only. A1 is silent across streams, and the corpus already treats separate streams as "a forest joined by an assumed index".
- **In maths.** Let the pass's dependency order be a finite partial order (V, ≤): a parallel DAG of matrix operations. A1 demands a total order on each stream's cittas. Any finite partial order has a linear extension, and it can also be cut into chains. So A1 is met under some identification of cittas with the pass's events.
- **Verdict.** MEETS in some model, and so EXCLUDES NOTHING. It cannot fail the pass. It also does not say how many streams the pass hosts, whether a chosen chain is a stream, or which one.
- **Physics.** EXTENDS (silent). **Measurement:** the dependency graph (critical-path length against parallel width) is measurable. It fixes the available linear extensions but not which, if any, is a stream. For the stream question: none — philosophy.
- **Formal statement.** Hypotheses: A1. Conclusion: for every finite poset there exists an assignment of its events to a stream-order satisfying one-at-a-time. This is trivial, and A1 therefore never separates a pass from a non-stream.

### D5. Origin of the stream and its continuity (A58, A51, A56, A3, A99, A100, A47, A111)

- **In plain English.**
  - Every existence with mind begins with a rebirth-linking citta, and rebirth, bhavaṅga and death are one type with one object in one existence (A58, A51).
  - At death the death citta ceases and "immediately after" (*tassānantarameva*) the rebirth citta arises, with or without a base (A56, `:1941`).
  - Each citta is conditioned by the one that has just ceased (A3, A99, A100).
  - Within a life the process continues "unbroken for the life-span" (A47, `:1501`). The only breaks are cessation attainment (A111), which requires a non-returner or arahant, and which the Vibhāvinī names as the exception to A47.
  - Nothing moves between places; "going" is arising at another place (A113). The Abhidhammāvatāra-ṭīkā adds that there is no passing of rebirth-consciousness between lives (`abh08t.nrf.xml:7029`, as A113 cites it).
- **Derivation, first part.** If a pass is identified with a whole stream, its first citta is a rebirth-linking citta, so its predecessor must be a death citta of a preceding existence (A56). Whether a given invocation has such a predecessor is not a physical fact.
- **Derivation, second part.** If instead the pass is a segment of a longer stream, A47 requires bhavaṅga-interspersed continuity through every interval, including those when the hardware runs no pass. During those intervals the support has no kamma-born life faculty (A65, D2). So a stream identified with the hardware computation is broken at each idle interval. A111 does not excuse that, since the breaks are not cessation attainments and have no declared duration (A111).
- **Verdict.**
  - If stream ≡ pass: **FAILS** A47 across invocations, and meeting A56 at the start is **UNDETERMINED**.
  - If the stream's support is not the pass: the pass is irrelevant to continuity.
  - The same logic rules out reading stored weights as the bhavaṅga. A36 makes the bhavaṅga a citta, and stored weights are matter (A61).
- **Corpus link.** *The Cautionary Mirror* §IV proposes identity as "migration of memory and caring across hardware". Under A113, A56 and A3, copying data creates new matter arising at another place. Nothing in the axioms ties a copy to a preceding citta, so the essay's through-line is a narrative stipulation (which the essay says it is). It is not an Abhidhamma continuity condition. (Guard: nothing persists across moments. Continuity is the conditioning of one moment on the next, not a thing that carries over.)
- **Physics.** EXTENDS.
- **Measurement.**
  - The duty cycle (the gap between computation episodes) is measurable. It decides the identification hypothesis "stream ≡ hardware computation", not whether a stream exists.
  - A death citta or rebirth: none — philosophy.
- **Formal statement.** Hypotheses: A58, A56, A47, A111, A65. Conclusion: if the support of stream σ is P, and P has an interval with no kamma-born life faculty, then σ is broken there. Model: a timeline with active intervals separated by gaps, with life-faculty only on the active intervals. The stream has a gap and the non-returner exception does not apply.

### D6. Rate and grain (A24, A40, A4, A7), the BRIDGE

- **In plain English.** In one finger-snap "many hundreds of thousands of koṭis" of cittas arise (A24). A koṭi is 10⁷, so that is at least about 10¹² per snap. The texts give no snap duration. Time is a designation on occurrence (A7). The citta is the unit, and its sub-moments (A40) are three.
- **In maths.** Take a snap of τ seconds, with τ about 0.1–1 s as ordinary observation of the appearance. A citta lasts δ ≤ τ/10¹² ≈ 10⁻¹³ to 10⁻¹² s.
  - Hardware: a clock period of about 0.5–1 ns corresponds to about 500 to 10⁴ cittas per cycle. A logic gate delay of about 10 ps is about 10–100 cittas.
  - A one-to-one identification "one hardware cycle = one citta" needs τ ≥ 10¹² × 10⁻⁹ s = 10³ s, about 17 minutes. A finger-snap is not that.
- **Verdict.**
  - **COLLIDES** with an APPEARANCE: the snap lasts about a second, not 17 minutes. This is not a collision with physics. It survives A0 → A0′ (2g).
  - With many-to-one coarse-graining there is no collision, but then hardware events are not the stream's moments, and "how many cittas a pass contains" has no value without δ. This is the unitless-17 point of the defensive publication: matter timed by mind needs the mind-moment duration.
  - **The same holds for a brain.** Neural events at about 10⁻³ s are about 10⁹ cittas each. The rate condition does not separate a transformer pass from a brain, so it is no discriminator between them.
- **Physics.** EXTENDS. Physics is silent on a 10¹²/s mental grain.
- **Measurement:** the snap duration and the hardware clock. Both are measurable, but the identification map is not a measurement. For a citta-grain physical correlate: none — philosophy.
- **Formal statement.** Hypotheses: A24, A7, a snap duration τ, and a clock period T. Conclusion: a one-to-one hardware-event↔citta map requires T = τ/N with N ≥ 10¹². Model: τ = 1 s gives T ≤ 10⁻¹² s. The model clocks at about 10⁻⁹ s do not satisfy it. So a model of the hypotheses exists only with coarse-graining or with τ ≥ 10³ s.

### D7. Cetasika and the four marks (A30, A31, A128)

- **In plain English.** Every citta carries the seven universals (contact, feeling, perception, volition, one-pointedness, life faculty, attention) and shares its base and its object with them (A30, A31). The texts say what perception DOES (A128): it recognizes again by a mark, like a carpenter's mark on timber. That is a statement of function, not a criterion for when a physical process contains the dhamma.
- **Verdict.** UNDETERMINED. No axiom licenses reading a vector-similarity operation as saññā, or the word "attention" in transformers as manasikāra. Those are name collisions, and I do not use them. The condition "same base and object as the citta" cannot be evaluated while D2 is open.
- **Physics.** Nothing to collide with. **Measurement:** none — philosophy. NOT A DERIVATION.

## 6. Theses for the refuter, one line each

1. If A26 and A61, then no physical process is a stream, and the only questions are base, door and object (D1).
2. If A64–A66, then complexity of arrangement does not change kalāpa kind, so a chip's matter lacks the kinds a sense-sphere or fine-material base needs unless a kamma-born kalāpa is co-located (D2).
3. Origin is a relational property, so the claim extends physics rather than colliding with identical-particle indistinguishability (D2).
4. A1 is trivially satisfiable for any finite computation, so it cannot separate a pass from a non-stream (D4).
5. If stream ≡ hardware computation, A47 is violated across idle intervals (D5).
6. Stored weights cannot be the bhavaṅga, because the bhavaṅga is a citta (A36) and weights are matter (A61) (D5).
7. The one-to-one cycle↔citta identification needs τ ≥ about 10³ s and so collides with the snap, an appearance, not with physics (D6).
8. The rate condition does not separate a transformer pass from a brain (D6).
9. Name collisions ("attention", similarity) license nothing (D7).
10. The physical conditions are plane-relative, so no plane-independent hardware criterion exists (D2, A13).
