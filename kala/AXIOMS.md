# AXIOMS — the one home of Abhidhamma mode's axioms

Every run of Abhidhamma mode (`runs/`) names its axioms by the IDs below and nothing else. The Lean
definitions they correspond to live in `Axioms.lean`. If an axiom changes, it changes HERE, and a run
that used the old wording keeps its own copy in its `PREREG.md`.

**Status** says how the citation was checked:
- **VERIFIED** — read at the cited line in the local Chaṭṭha Saṅgāyana (CST) files, or fetched.
- **RECALLED** — cited from knowledge, not yet read. A run may use a RECALLED axiom only if its
  pre-registration says so (`prereg.sh --allow-recalled`), and the run report must repeat the word.
- **RULED** — a founder ruling of this program, not a textual claim.

**Tier** — Piṭaka · commentarial (aṭṭhakathā) · sub-commentarial (ṭīkā) · manual (Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha) ·
manual's commentary (Abhidhammatthavibhāvinī) · ours. `[X]` marks a cross-tradition import. CST file layers: `*.mul` mūla
(Piṭaka) · `*.att` aṭṭhakathā · `*.tik` ṭīkā · `*.nrf` other (the Saṅgaha is `abh07t.nrf.xml` lines 13–3497; its Vibhāvinī
starts at line 3505). Line numbers are in the UTF-16 CST files as decoded by `cst.py`; ⚠️ a plain `grep` over them returns nothing.
**Text version:** line numbers are as of `VipassanaTech/tipitaka-xml` `romn/` @ `05d5d3c` (2026-09-16); the local copy is
kept byte-identical by `cst-sync.py`, which names any axiom whose cited file later changes.

## Coverage — what "all" means here (surveyed 2026-09-30)

The Abhidhamma states no axioms. It gives a classification of what is real and a calculus of how one thing conditions
another. An axiom here is a law of that system that a derivation can reason from. **The index is the Abhidhammattha-saṅgaha's
nine chapters, all read in full on 2026-09-30**, with each law lifted to the Piṭaka wherever the Piṭaka line was found.
There is one row per law or per enumerated list. IDs are never renumbered; new rows continue the A-series.

| Saṅgaha chapter | Rows |
|---|---|
| — the window | A0, A0′ |
| 1–2 what is real · consciousness · mental factors | A26–A32 |
| 3 feeling · roots · functions · doors · objects · bases | A33–A39 |
| 4 the cognitive process, and mind-time | A1, A4, A24, A25, A40–A47 |

| 6 matter · space · nibbāna (+ breath, from the Visuddhimagga) | A5, A6, A9, A12, A15, A60–A70, A114, K |
| 7 categories | A71–A74 |
| 8 conditionality: dependent origination · the 24 conditions · the three times · concepts · motion | A3, A7, A8, C, A75–A104, A113 |
| 9 meditation · the path · cessation | A105–A112 |
| outside the Saṅgaha: cosmology and beginnings | A2, A2b, A10, A14, A16–A23 |

**Not in this pass (the declared aperture).** The Kathāvatthu's debates other than the five cited here (1.6, 2.5, 6.6, 8.2,
22.10, of about 219); the Yamaka; the Dhātukathā; the Puggalapaññatti; the Dhammasaṅgaṇī's matrix of 22 triads and 100 dyads; the
Paṭṭhāna's permutations of the 24 conditions across that matrix (the conditions themselves are in); the Vibhaṅga beyond what
is cited; and the commentaries' further numbers. These are the next layer, not finished work.

### Where a derivation can grip

Most rows classify. A physical derivation needs a row that carries one of the following, and a measurement on the other side:
- **A number**: A4 (17) · A24 (rate of mind) · A41 (14 + 2 + 1) · A44 (7, 5 or 1 javanas) · A49–A50 (life-spans) · A60 (28) · A66 (21 kalāpas) · A14, A16, A17, A19, A20, A23 (yojanas).
- **An order or an adjacency**: A3, A82, A99, A100 (the citta that has just ceased conditions the present one) · A56 (death to rebirth with nothing between) · A59 (no intermediate existence) · A111–A112 (a gap in the stream that the order does not register — the texts' own position, Vism XVII §598).
- **Simultaneity or symmetry**: A30 (four marks) · A83–A84 (co-nascence, mutuality) · A88 (a later state supports an earlier-arisen one).
- **What perception can reach**: A37 (sense-door objects are present only) · A41 (the object is already at least one mind-moment old when it enters the door) · A63 (eye and ear need no contact) · A87 (organ and object must already exist).
- **What the outside world is made of**: A64 (temperature is the only origin of matter outside beings) · A66 (only two kalāpa kinds occur outside beings).
- **Time and space as concepts, and the three times**: A7, A8, A9, A103, A104.
- **Motion**: A113 (nothing moves; "going" is arising at another place) — with A4 and A24 it bounds a speed, one place-step per arising.
