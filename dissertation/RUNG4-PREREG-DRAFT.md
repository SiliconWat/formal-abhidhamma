## 4 · What each outcome will mean

- **P-FA7 inside:** compression is a property of axis-built rule systems on these axes. The counts check's
  compression is then expected, and the dissertation says so: the finding is the consistency of the two
  methods of chapter 2, not the compression.
- **P-FA7 below 5:** the text's rules are more economical than random rules of the same shape.
  **Above 95:** less economical.
- **P-FA9 holds:** distinct tables can share every projection (the Gale–Ryser fact the counts check
  concedes), but no pair of rule-shaped errors does. The key is then sharper against rule errors than
  against arbitrary tables, and the dissertation states exactly that scope.
- **P-FA9 fails:** every surviving pair is listed as a blind spot of the text's figures, cells the text's
  totals do not pin, and chapter 8 reports them.

## 6 · What is not tested

(rung 2's breaks cover those by hand); any second-order effect beyond pairs.
