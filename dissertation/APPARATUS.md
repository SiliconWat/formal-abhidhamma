# Apparatus: how every figure is cited

## The rule

Every count in the dissertation names its source three ways: the CST paragraph (VRI `romn/abh07t`, chapter and
§), the second printing it was cross-checked against, and, where the comparison exists, the Khmer edition's
volume and page. A figure the text does not state is never keyed.

## The CST side (done)

`sangaha/AnswerKey.lean` already cites the CST paragraph for every keyed figure: per-factor counts §13–§19;
unwholesome §20–§27; beautiful §28–§32; supramundane §36–§37; sublime §38–§39; sense-sphere beautiful
§40–§41; unwholesome groupings §43–§51; rootless §53–§58; chapter 3 §3–§17. Cross-checked against a second
printing (the Pāli with Nārada's translation). Bodhi's Tables 2.2–2.4 are the printed chart.

## The Khmer side (owed)

- **The printing.** The Khmer Tipiṭaka of the Buddhist Institute, Phnom Penh. Which printing the transcription
  follows, and its year, must be identified from the volumes themselves (volumes 77–80 hold the material
  read so far).
- **Volume and page per figure,** beyond the one passage compared in rung 3.
- **Open readings:** the long *ī* of *vīriya-* at two positions (read from the scan at moderate confidence);
  the siglum *o. ma.*, read as Burmese without the edition's abbreviation key.
- **Independence:** the CST is the Burmese recension and the Khmer apparatus cites Burmese readings, so the two
  are not independent witnesses. The dissertation says so wherever it reports agreement.

## What may be published

No Khmer-edition text is committed to this public repository: only counts, positions and single romanized
terms. The reproduction question is open, and the Buddhist Institute's blessing is sought. Any use of the
scans names their donors: Kang Huychan and family · Lok Kru Aggapaṇḍita But Savong · Srong Chanda ·
5000-years.org.
